# Capa ML — Style Guide
*Last updated: 2026-06-22*

---

## Section 1 — Cover Image Fundamentals

ML cover images (position 1 in listing gallery) must:
- Show the product clearly, as the main subject
- Contain NO text, NO watermarks, NO contact info, NO logos
- Work at 60×60px thumbnail size (mobile search grid)
- Be square (1:1 ratio), minimum 1500×1500px
- Have product occupying at least 60% of the frame

The cover's single job: make the buyer click before reading anything.

---

## Section 2 — Thumbnail Legibility Rules

Every cover must succeed at two sizes simultaneously:
- **Full size (1500px):** beautiful composition, product detail, premium feel
- **60×60px thumbnail:** product still instantly recognizable, strong contrast, no visual noise

To ensure thumbnail legibility:
- High contrast between product and background is non-negotiable
- Product fills at least 60% of frame (70% preferred)
- Clean product silhouette — complex or busy backgrounds destroy thumbnail legibility
- No competing visual elements at frame edges that become noise at small size
- Test mentally: "If someone sees this as a tiny square, do they know what this is?"

## Section 3 — Style Execution Guide

### Recorte Dramático

**Concept:** Pure product drama. No context, no props, no people — just the product looking as premium as possible.

**Composition:**
- 3/4 angle view (not straight front) — shows depth and dimension
- Product fills 65-75% of frame
- Soft shadow beneath product for natural grounding
- No props, no people, no lifestyle context

**Background:**
| Product Category | Background Color |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Electronics | Deep navy (#0D1B2A) or charcoal (#1A1A1A) |
| Home appliances | Warm gray (#C8C8C8) or slate (#6B7280) |
| Home decor | Sage green (#B5C4B1) or warm beige (#E8DCC8) |
| Clothing | Warm off-white (#FAF7F2) or soft taupe (#C9B99A) |
| Unknown | Charcoal (#2C2C2C) |

**Lighting:** Dramatic side lighting with directional source, visible shadows, subtle highlight on product edges.

**DALL-E prompt pattern:**
> "Professional product photography of [product description from photos], 3/4 angle view, dramatic side studio lighting, product against [background color] background, product fills 70% of frame, clean silhouette, photorealistic, studio quality, square 1:1 format, no text no logos"

---

### Ambiente Recortado

**Concept:** Product on clean background, but with exactly 1 contextual element that tells the buyer where and how this product belongs in their life.

**Composition:**
- Background: white (#FFFFFF) or very light gray (#F5F5F5)
- Product centered, filling 55-65% of frame
- ONE contextual element maximum — choose the most natural for the product type:
  - Appliance → edge of clean kitchen countertop, or a simple wooden surface
  - Electronics → partial human hand in relaxed grip (bottom edge of frame only)
  - Home decor → corner of a styled minimal surface (one small neutral-colored object nearby)
  - Textile/clothing → clean flat lay on white linen or cotton surface
  - Small gadget → neutral desk surface edge, cable neatly placed

**The contextual element must NOT compete with the product — it should feel natural and barely noticed.**

**Lighting:** Soft, even studio lighting. No harsh shadows. Gentle natural-light feel.

**Mood:** Clean, aspirational. "This product belongs in my home."

**DALL-E prompt pattern:**
> "Professional product photography of [product description], centered on white background, [contextual element] visible in corner/edge of frame, soft even studio lighting, clean and minimal composition, photorealistic, product fills 60% of frame, square 1:1 format, no text no logos"

---

### Destaque Emocional

**Concept:** The buyer feels the product's benefit before consciously reading specs. Upper portion of image conveys the emotion/benefit; product is anchored at the bottom.

**Composition:**
- Product: bottom 30-40% of frame, centered
- Upper 60-70%: abstract visual representing the product's CORE BENEFIT

**Benefit → Visual mapping:**
| Product Benefit | Visual Direction |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Warmth / heating | Soft orange-amber bokeh glow, radiating warm light |
| Cooling / freshness | Light blue gradient, subtle motion blur, airy feel |
| Bright lighting | Starburst light effect, warm-to-cool tone gradient |
| Organization / order | Clean white space, geometric clean lines, structured |
| Power / performance | Dark dramatic shadows, energy lines, high contrast |
| Comfort / softness | Pastel tones, blurred soft textures, gentle light |
| Kitchen / food | Warm aroma wisps (subtle steam), honey-warm tones |

**Transition:** The two zones (benefit visual + product) must flow naturally into each other — use soft gradient or light fall-off, never a hard border.

**DALL-E prompt pattern:**
> "Product photo of [product description] placed in the bottom-center third of the frame, [benefit visual description] filling the upper two-thirds of the image, smooth gradient transition between both zones, professional editorial photography, photorealistic, square 1:1 format, no text no logos"

---

### Contraste Competitivo

**Concept:** Deliberate visual contrast against the typical style in this product's category on ML. Designed to stand out in the search grid where a buyer sees 15+ thumbnails simultaneously.

**Strategy:**
| If typical category style is... | Use instead... |
|--------------------------------|----------------|
| White/light background | Dark background (charcoal, navy, slate) |
| Dark/dramatic background | Pure white, ultra-clean, minimal |
| Straight front view | 3/4 angle or top-down overhead view |
| Lifestyle with people | Pure product, no people, clean studio |
| Pure product shot | Minimal lifestyle element (hand, surface) |

**Rules:**
- Apply the contrast FULLY — a half-contrast approach reads as inconsistent, not distinct
- The product must still comply with ML specs (clearly visible, fills 60%+ of frame)
- High contrast between product and background is mandatory at thumbnail size
- Goal: in a row of 6 similar-looking thumbnails, this one stops the eye

**DALL-E prompt pattern:**
> "Professional product photography of [product description], [contrasting visual direction], [contrasting background], product fills 65% of frame, [contrasting lighting style], photorealistic, studio quality, square 1:1 format, no text no logos"

## Section 5 — What NEVER to Include in a Cover

- Text of any kind (ML policy prohibits text on cover/position-1 image)
- Watermarks or seller branding
- Competitor logos or brand names
- Phone numbers, email, WhatsApp, social media handles, URLs
- Certification seals not officially held by the seller
- Multiple products (unless it's a kit sold together)
- Low-resolution or pixelated visuals
- Decorative borders or frames that obscure the product
