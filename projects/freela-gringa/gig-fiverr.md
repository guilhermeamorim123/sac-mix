## Descrição

```
Your customers message on WhatsApp at 22:00. You reply at 09:00 the next day.
By then, many have already booked somewhere else.

I build WhatsApp AI assistants that answer instantly, around the clock — in
your language, with your prices, your opening hours and your rules.

WHAT YOU GET

A working WhatsApp assistant that:
- Answers the 20-30 questions that make up most of your inbox (price, hours,
  location, availability, how a service works, payment methods)
- Understands natural language, not keyword menus — customers write the way
  they normally write
- Hands over to a human when the question is outside its scope, with the
  conversation history attached so nobody repeats themselves
- Captures the lead: name, what they wanted, when they asked

BUILT ON THE OFFICIAL API

I use the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API from Meta. I do not use
browser automation or unofficial bridges — those violate WhatsApp's terms and
can get your business number banned. Your number stays yours.

GDPR-CONSCIOUS SETUP

Built for European businesses: the assistant states clearly that it is
automated, collects only what is needed to answer, and I document what data
is stored and where. You get a short written summary you can hand to whoever
handles your privacy policy.

WORKS IN YOUR LANGUAGE

German, Spanish, Italian, French, Portuguese, Dutch, English. The assistant
replies in the language your customer writes in.

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

- Your most common customer questions (or just forward me 20 real chats)
- Prices, hours, address, and anything the assistant must never say
- Access to your WhatsApp Business account for the connection

No technical knowledge needed on your side. I walk you through the connection
step by step.

NOT A GOOD FIT IF

- You want it to take payments inside the chat
- You need it connected to a booking system I have never seen — message me
  first and I will tell you honestly whether I can do it
- You expect it to handle complaints or medical questions. It should not, and
  I will set it to hand those to a human
```

## FAQ do anúncio

```
Q: Will this get my WhatsApp number banned?
A: No. I use the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API from Meta, which is the
   supported way to automate a business number. Sellers who use browser
   automation put your number at risk. I do not.

Q: Do I need to be technical?
A: No. You send me your questions and prices. I do the setup and walk you
   through connecting your account.

Q: What does it cost to run after you build it?
A: Meta charges per conversation and includes a free tier each month. Small
   businesses often stay inside or near it. The AI itself is a few cents per
   conversation. I show you the real numbers before we start, so there are
   no surprises.

Q: Can it answer in German?
A: Yes — German, Spanish, Italian, French, Portuguese, Dutch and English. It
   replies in whatever language the customer writes in.

Q: What happens when it does not know something?
A: It says so plainly and hands the conversation to a human, with the history
   attached. I would rather it admit a gap than invent an answer — a chatbot
   that guesses costs you more than one that transfers.

Q: Can I change the answers later myself?
A: Yes. The answers live in a simple sheet you can edit. I show you how.
```
