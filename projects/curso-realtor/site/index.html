The part most prompt packs skip

The guardrails are written in

Every prompt that touches a client carries the instructions that keep you out of trouble:

  • Never invent a local fact. The model writes; it does not research.
  • Delete rather than guess a detail about a lead they never gave you.
  • Never promise an outcome you don't control — no predictions about rates, prices or timelines.
  • Fair housing note in the module where it actually matters.
  • Label every generated image. A buyer should never be surprised at the door.

You're the one who signs the email. That should be built into the tool, not left to you to remember at 11pm.
